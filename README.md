# 🌌 UNRAVEL

> *The humans are gone. Their memories aren't.*

**UNRAVEL** is a narrative-driven 3D exploration game built with **jMonkeyEngine**.  
The player takes the role of an alien explorer arriving on an abandoned Earth after receiving a mysterious signal.

There are no humans left to explain what happened.

Instead, scattered objects, fragmented memories, environmental traces, and an abandoned machine named **MUSAFIR** tell their story.

The player's mission is simple:

**Observe. Collect. Understand. Decide.**

---

## 🎮 About the Game

UNRAVEL explores humanity through the eyes of someone who was never human.

The alien arrives on an apparently lifeless Earth with a set of objectives designed to determine what happened to its inhabitants and whether humanity was ultimately worth contacting.

While exploring an abandoned university campus, the player discovers objects left behind by humans.

Picking up certain objects triggers **memories from the past**.

Some are funny.

Some are ordinary.

Some reveal kindness.

Others reveal the environmental destruction, conflict, and choices that eventually shaped humanity's fate.

As more fragments are collected, the alien gradually reconstructs a civilization it never got the chance to meet.

---

## 👽 The Player

You play as an alien explorer investigating Earth after detecting an unexplained signal.

The player can:

- Explore the abandoned environment
- Discover collectible objects
- Trigger memories of humanity
- Complete exploration objectives
- Interact with **MUSAFIR**
- Unlock new abilities
- Piece together what happened to Earth
- Reach the final **Moment of Truth**

---

## 🛰️ MUSAFIR

During the journey, the alien discovers the source of the mysterious signal:

**MUSAFIR**

An abandoned machine carrying information left behind by humanity.

Its message begins with:

> **"If you are hearing me, then humans failed."**

MUSAFIR becomes one of the alien's most important connections to the civilization it is trying to understand.

But even MUSAFIR cannot give the alien a simple answer.

Were humans a failed species?

Or were they something more complicated?

---

## 🧩 Memory System

Throughout the map, the player discovers objects connected to moments from humanity's past.

Each object acts as a fragment of memory.

These memories range from everyday campus life to much larger events involving:

- 🌧️ Climate and environmental change
- 🌳 Deforestation
- 💧 Resource scarcity
- 🐾 Humanity's relationship with animals
- 🧪 Animal research
- ☢️ Nuclear destruction
- ❤️ Human compassion and attempts to protect life
- 🎓 Ordinary moments of university life

Together, these fragments slowly change the alien's understanding of humanity.

---

## 🎯 Mission Objectives

The alien's investigation revolves around several major objectives:

- Search for evidence of human civilization
- Investigate signs of biological life
- Understand human behavior
- Observe humanity's impact on its environment
- Recover fragments of the past
- Locate the mysterious signal
- Determine what happened to humanity
- Decide whether humanity was worth contacting

The objectives are gradually completed as the player explores and collects memories.

---

## 🌠 The Moment of Truth

After enough memories have been recovered, the alien returns to MUSAFIR.

One final question remains:

> **Did humans survive?**

But survival alone isn't the real question.

The alien must consider everything it has witnessed — destruction, absurdity, mistakes, kindness, resilience and compassion — before confronting the final question:

> **Even if humanity survived, were they worth contacting?**

The journey concludes beneath a darkened sky as a **meteor shower** marks the end of UNRAVEL.

## 🚀 Running UNRAVEL

### Requirements

Make sure you have:

- Java installed
- Gradle configured
- A GPU supporting the required OpenGL version

### Clone the repository

```bash
git clone https://github.com/Shira-yuki23/Unravel.git
cd Unravel
```

### Run using Gradle

```bash
gradlew run
```

On Linux/macOS:

```bash
./gradlew run
```

You can also open the project directly in **IntelliJ IDEA** and run the desktop launcher.

## 💭 Why "UNRAVEL"?

The player isn't given humanity's story.

They have to **unravel it**.

One abandoned object.

One memory.

One contradiction.

One human moment at a time.

The game isn't simply about discovering how humanity disappeared.

It's about discovering **who humans were**.

---

## 🌍 A Story About Humanity

Humans damaged their world.

Humans fought.

Humans ignored warnings.

But humans also protected animals, helped strangers, created memories, laughed at ridiculous things, built communities and tried to repair what they had broken.

UNRAVEL deliberately leaves those contradictions intact.

Because perhaps understanding humanity was never supposed to produce a simple answer.
